## ERP Overview

- ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) is a centralized software system that helps organizations integrate and manage their core business processes in a single platform. Systems like SAP S/4HANA enable seamless coordination between departments such as Sales, Finance, Procurement, Manufacturing, and Human Resources, ensuring real-time data visibility and efficient decision-making.

- An ERP system eliminates data silos by maintaining a single source of truth, allowing different business functions to share and access consistent information. It supports end-to-end business processes—for example, from order creation to delivery and billing, or from procurement to payment.

- ERP systems are essential for organizations aiming to streamline operations, reduce costs, and improve overall business performance.

## SAP Overview

- SAP (Systems, Applications, and Products in Data Processing) is one of the world’s leading enterprise software solutions developed by SAP. It provides integrated applications that help organizations manage their business operations efficiently across various functions such as Finance, Sales, Procurement, Manufacturing, and Human Resources.

- SAP systems are built on the concept of real-time data processing and integration, enabling all departments within an organization to work on a single, unified platform. Modern solutions like SAP S/4HANA use advanced in-memory computing to deliver faster performance, simplified data models, and enhanced user experience through tools like SAP Fiori.

- SAP supports end-to-end business processes—for example, from customer order creation to delivery and billing, or from procurement to payment—ensuring seamless data flow and improved decision-making.

- This introductory topic helps learners understand the foundation of SAP systems and their importance in modern enterprises.
